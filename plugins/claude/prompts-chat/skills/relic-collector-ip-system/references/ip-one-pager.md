# Relic Collector One Pager

## Logline

After the disaster, the black market discovers that the most valuable things in sealed buildings are not gold or weapons, but anchored relics. A black-market demolition crew breaks into a sealed apartment block to steal an old tabletop fan, only to trigger the building's ownership rules: every stolen object begins collecting its debt. The relic collector arrives with white gloves, sealing tags, and one advantage the thieves do not have: he knows the dead still leave rules behind.

## Active IP Promise

- ordinary objects become dangerous because they remember ownership;
- every case starts with a rule-reversal payoff before the emotional truth is revealed;
- the protagonist wins through procedure, object knowledge, and restraint rather than combat;
- black-market greed gives the audience a clear target for satisfaction;
- each relic case stands alone while expanding a larger city of awakened ownership chains.

## First-Season Spine

Title direction: `别碰那台风扇`.

Season 1 is a 20-minute first-season pilot built around one sealed apartment block:

1. black-market thieves enter the building and steal relics;
2. an old fan is revealed as an A-level public relic, not a household keepsake;
3. each stolen object triggers a clear rule and reverses the theft;
4. after the satisfying reversals, the audience learns the fan once helped the whole building survive a blackout heat wave;
5. the collector chooses on-site sealing instead of taking the fan away, because it is the building's ownership switch.

## Tone

- professional rule thriller;
- cold but satisfying;
- restrained aftershock, not immediate tearjerker;
- municipal recovery realism;
- ordinary-object spectacle, not monster spectacle.

## Anti-Regression Rule

Do not default back to a quiet doorstep-return tearjerker. The current audience promise is:

> 先爽，后疼: steal the wrong object, trigger the rule, watch the black market pay, then reveal why the object mattered.
