# Proof Shots

Use these as the Seedance2 validation set for the active first-season direction.

Current Echoon constraint from prior testing: prefer `16:9 / 720p / 5s` units. Do not force 7s or 8s unless account credit and model pricing allow it.

## Proof Shot List

1. `S01 Debt is not cleared`
   - black-market thief tries to escape, but the backpack and stolen objects drag themselves back into the building; the collector stands still in the corridor.
2. `S02 Fan opens the account`
   - white glove restores the child's bus card to the fan guard; the fan trembles and nearby relics begin to move.
3. `S03 Doorplate loop`
   - a thief exits a corridor and lands back at the stolen doorplate's original door.
4. `S04 Radio exposes the black market`
   - a stolen radio refuses to shut off and broadcasts the black-market channel through the room.
5. `S05 Toy car drags stolen goods`
   - a toy car pulls keys and small objects along a child's drawn route.
6. `S06 Building recognizes owners`
   - multiple ordinary objects return to their correct positions in precise paths.
7. `S07 On-site sealing`
   - the collector leaves the fan by the window and seals it in place instead of taking it away.

## First Batch

After script approval and Image2 asset approval, generate only:

1. `S01` to prove the hook;
2. `S02` to prove the core fan rule;
3. `S06` to prove the final scale.

Expand only after those three pass.

## Proof-Shot Gate

- each shot has one readable action;
- no shot depends on explanatory subtitles;
- the first frame is a true Image2 first frame, not a generic portrait or design board;
- black-market thieves do not become generic soldiers or survivors;
- the collector does not run, panic, or perform action-hero combat;
- relic movement must read as `returning to place`, not random supernatural floating;
- no text, watermark, UI, or garbled signage may control the shot.

If the same weakness repeats across two rerolls, stop generating and return to asset or premise revision instead of brute-force rerolling.
