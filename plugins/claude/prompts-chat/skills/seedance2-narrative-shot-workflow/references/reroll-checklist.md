# Reroll Checklist

Reroll when the shot fails one of these:

1. The first frame is not respected.
2. Identity drifts.
3. Unwanted text appears.
4. The gesture becomes anatomically unstable.
5. The emotional beat lands too early or too late.
6. Dialogue lines are missing, reordered, or expanded.
7. Sound design fights the scene.

Keep when the result adds believable life without breaking continuity:

- a slight pause;
- a better glance;
- a stronger prop touch;
- more convincing body hesitation;
- environmental micro-noise that fits the setting.
