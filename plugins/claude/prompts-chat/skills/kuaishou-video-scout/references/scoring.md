# Kuaishou Idea Scoring

Score 1-5. Prefer the highest total only when compliance risk is acceptable.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook strength | vague | understandable | immediate curiosity or benefit |
| Audience desire | unclear | niche but real | obvious pain/desire |
| Visual proof | mostly text | some visual | strong before/after, demo, face, product, or result |
| Production speed | new assets needed | moderate edit | can publish or edit today |
| Account fit | off-theme | adjacent | directly reinforces positioning |
| Risk | high risk | manageable | low risk |

## Candidate Format

```text
素材:
角度:
开头3秒:
为什么现在发:
缺什么:
分数:
风险:
```
