# Douyin Selection Rubric

Score each candidate out of 100.

| Dimension | Points | What Good Looks Like |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First 3 seconds | 20 | Immediate conflict, result, transformation, surprise, or clear benefit |
| Viewer relevance | 15 | One obvious audience with a felt pain or desire |
| Visual proof | 20 | Footage visibly supports the promise without relying on text alone |
| Retention path | 15 | Natural reason to watch to the end: reveal, steps, comparison, before/after |
| Production ease | 10 | Can be edited quickly with existing assets |
| Differentiation | 10 | Not a generic claim; has a personal detail, niche, or unusual frame |
| Compliance safety | 10 | Avoids risky claims, platform-sensitive themes, and misleading framing |

## Output Format

```markdown
候选排名：
1. 素材/主题 - 分数 - 选择理由 - 最大风险
2. ...

最终选择：
备用选择：
需要补拍/补资料：
```
