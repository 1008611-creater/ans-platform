# Award Bar

Use this file when the goal is not "做一个不错的 AI 短片", but "做一个能争第一的原创 IP 电影级短片".

## The bar

The film must clear all six:

1. `One-look world`
   - One frame must immediately reveal that the film belongs to a specific world, not a generic AI style soup.
2. `One-line hook`
   - The premise should be understandable and intriguing in one sentence.
3. `One iconic protagonist signal`
   - silhouette, mask, costume, tool, vehicle, ritual, or job role.
4. `One emotional wound`
   - grief, guilt, desire, shame, longing, duty, fear, or tenderness.
5. `One unforgettable image`
   - at least one shot that people can remember and repost as a still.
6. `One expansion path`
   - the IP should obviously be able to become a series, game, or larger world.

## Automatic disqualifiers

- generic cyberpunk;
- over-reliance on spectacle with no human feeling;
- too many speaking faces in tight realism;
- giant crowd war scenes;
- empty lore without one central emotional spine;
- derivative robot-western wasteland after Mx-Shell.

## Practical production fit

The winning idea should also fit Seedance2 and Image2:

- controlled number of recurring characters;
- costumes or identity anchors that stabilize consistency;
- environments strong enough to impress, simple enough to repeat;
- scenes that can be broken into 5-second dramatic units;
- audio and gesture more important than complicated choreography.
