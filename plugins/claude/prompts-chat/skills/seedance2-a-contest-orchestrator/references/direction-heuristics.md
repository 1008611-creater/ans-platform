# Direction Heuristics

## What wins now

From current platform signals and public case studies, the strongest AI narrative works tend to combine:

- complete world design;
- a memorable symbol or profession;
- local cultural specificity or emotionally legible human context;
- restrained but intentional storytelling;
- strong post-selection and editorial taste;
- AI used as invisible craft rather than visible gimmick.

## What probably loses at the top

- generic myth monster compilations;
- all-style-no-substance sci-fi reels;
- "look how realistic AI is" as the only point;
- plot systems that require too many extras, crowds, or dialogue-heavy realism;
- direct copies of the tone or subject of recent breakout hits.

## High-potential archetype

The strongest original-IP structure for this contest is usually:

- a cinematic profession or institution;
- one iconic worker/protagonist;
- one rule-based world;
- one intimate emotional case;
- one twist that expands the IP.

Examples of structure, not titles:

- memory salvage bureau;
- lantern courier between worlds;
- forbidden relic recovery unit;
- dream forensics office;
- post-disaster ritual service worker.
