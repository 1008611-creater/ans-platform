# Fruit Video Patterns

## Pattern A: Beauty Host Eating And Small Dance

Use with Wan action transfer.

```markdown
画面：年轻水果主播，干净自然妆，手里拿着当季水果，桌上有切开的果肉。
动作：前2秒先咬一口或举到镜头前，再做轻松小幅舞步，始终让水果留在画面中心。
重点：表情真实，水果汁水/果肉清楚，动作轻快但不夜店。
```

Positive prompt:

```text
best quality, natural mobile livestream, Chinese fruit host, holding fresh fruit close to camera, bright clean fruit stall or orchard packing table, appetizing fruit flesh, playful light dance, natural smile, realistic skin, stable handheld phone framing, vertical short video
```

Negative prompt:

```text
overexposed, nightclub, sensual pose, fruit disappearing, deformed hands, extra fingers, wrong face, text watermark, logo, blurry fruit, plastic skin, fake CGI, messy background, multiple people blocking fruit
```

## Pattern B: Orchard Origin Proof

Use with real footage first. Use generated clips only for transition or illustrative shots.

```markdown
画面：果树、采摘、满筐水果、分拣、装箱。
钩子：这筐今天刚下树 / 这批为什么看着普通但更甜。
CTA：要我拍称重和打包过程就在评论区说。
```

## Pattern C: Plain Elder Pitch

Use with LTX digital human when the user has a suitable image and voice.

Identity prompt:

```text
An elderly Chinese fruit seller standing in an orchard packing area, holding a bamboo basket of fresh fruit, plain cotton shirt, kind sincere expression, weathered but healthy face, natural daylight, real rural fruit origin atmosphere, mobile phone short video style, honest direct-selling tone
```

Local action segments:

```text
She gently lifts the fruit basket toward the camera and nods with a sincere small smile, her eyes glancing down at the fruit then back to the viewer | She picks up one fruit and turns it slowly to show the peel and size, speaking calmly like explaining to a neighbor | She cuts or opens one fruit to reveal the flesh, leaning closer so the camera can see the juicy texture | She points to the boxes behind her and gestures to the sorting area, explaining that bad fruit is picked out | She holds the basket with both hands and gives a warm simple closing invitation
```

## Pattern D: Host Explains Price Reason

Use when viewers ask why price is high or low.

```markdown
0-3s: 主播直接拿果说“这个价不是乱报的”。
3-7s: 展示果径、成熟度、产地采摘。
7-11s: 展示分拣/坏果/包装成本。
11-15s: 轻 CTA：想看成本拆给你看。
```
