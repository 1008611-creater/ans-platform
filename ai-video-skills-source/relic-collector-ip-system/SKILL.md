# Relic Collector IP System

Use this skill when the project direction is the `Relic Collector` IP line.

## Current Direction

The active version is a post-apocalyptic professional rule-thriller:

> 黑市偷楼，遗物开账，整栋楼认主。先爽，后疼。

The old pure tearjerker version, where the collector quietly returns a relic at a doorstep, is no longer the default. Do not revert to it unless the user explicitly asks.

## What This Skill Owns

1. the world premise;
2. the institution, black-market ecology, and recovery rules;
3. the protagonist identity system;
4. the first-season case template;
5. the recurring symbol set;
6. the project's asset and proof-shot priorities.

## Guardrails

- Lead with rule-reversal satisfaction, then reveal emotional residue.
- Keep the cast tight: collector, black-market crew, one season antagonist, and relic traces.
- Keep the prop logic exact; every relic needs a visible rule, not just mood.
- Let the relic carry emotion after it has already produced a clear action payoff.
- Keep the world cold, controlled, and municipal, but not emotionally dead.
- Avoid broad apocalypse spectacle, zombie hordes, monster logic, neon cyberpunk, and large battle scenes.
- Use ordinary objects as high-control AI surfaces: fan, bus card, doorplate, radio, toy car, keys, lunchbox, umbrella.
- Do not over-explain grief. Show the rule, then let one ordinary detail reveal why the object matters.
