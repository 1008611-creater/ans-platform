# Premise And Scenes

## First-season premise

A black-market demolition crew enters a disaster-sealed apartment block to steal anchored relics for resale. Their target is an old tabletop fan wrapped with a child's bus card and a voice chip. They believe the fan can summon valuable relics from the whole building. The relic collector knows the real rule: the fan does not find goods, it restores ownership. Once the fan is touched, stolen doorplates, radios, toy cars, keys, lunchboxes, and other ordinary objects begin returning to their correct places, and the thieves are pulled into the building's debt ledger.

The season's engine is not grief first. It is rule satisfaction first, emotional residue second.

## 2.5-minute contest / trailer premise

Open on a black-market thief escaping a sealed building. His backpack drags itself back inside as stolen objects crawl out and return down the corridor. The relic collector stands in the doorway wearing white inspection gloves: `The debt is not cleared.` Cut back ten minutes. The thieves break into the building, steal household objects, and find the old fan. When the fan is switched on, the collector restores the child's bus card to the fan guard. The fan opens the account: stolen objects return to their rooms, the thieves' hidden goods are exposed, and the building begins recognizing owners. The final hook expands from one fan to one building, then to a city of sealed zones waking up.

## Must-land scenes

1. `Debt is not cleared`
   - a thief tries to escape, but his stolen objects drag themselves back into the sealed building.
2. `Fan opens the account`
   - the collector returns the child's bus card to the fan guard, and the room's stolen objects begin moving back to their correct positions.
3. `Doorplate loop`
   - the thief who stole a doorplate keeps exiting into the same corridor until the plate returns to the original door.
4. `Radio exposes the black market`
   - a stolen radio broadcasts the thieves' channel to the whole building.
5. `Toy car drags stolen goods`
   - a toy car pulls keys and small objects along a child's route back to the bedroom.
6. `Debt counter`
   - the antagonist's black-market counter lights up, proving he has stolen from many sealed sites before.
7. `Building recognizes owners`
   - doorplates, keys, lunchboxes, umbrellas, radios, and toys return in precise paths, creating a rules-based set piece rather than an explosion.
8. `On-site sealing`
   - the collector does not take the A-level fan away; he seals it in place because it is the building's ownership switch.

## Tail hook

The collector leaves the building. A canister on his shoulder trembles. Across the sealed district, old appliances begin lighting up one by one. The case was not the first awakened relic. It was the first awakened building.
