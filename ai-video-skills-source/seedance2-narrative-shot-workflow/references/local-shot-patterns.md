# Local Shot Patterns

## Pattern 1: front-loaded ban block

The strongest local prompts state the text ban immediately:

- no subtitles;
- no dialogue text;
- no translation text;
- no watermark;
- allow only specific prop text if needed.

This reduces cleanup later.

## Pattern 2: first-frame lock before timed action

Useful structure:

1. shot purpose and absolute ban;
2. strict first-frame/style lock;
3. camera rule;
4. timed beats;
5. audio and dialogue rules;
6. repeated reminder about no subtitles/text.

## Pattern 3: simple camera, specific acting

Local prompts performed better when the camera remained simple while the actor behavior carried the scene:

- flattening paper;
- glancing at another person;
- lifting eyes slowly;
- pausing before writing;
- returning toward camera to pick up a phone.

## Pattern 4: explicit soundtrack bans

If the scene is dialogue- or ambience-driven, say:

- no BGM;
- no melody;
- no emotional score;
- only room tone, paper, footsteps, machine sounds, or dialogue.
