# Douyin Fruit Commerce Strategy

## Overview

Use this skill to turn fruit-selling ideas into a repeatable account system. The goal is not one viral clip; it is a content factory with stable personas, proof scenes, product trust, and conversion hooks.

## Core Position

Default to this account thesis:

```markdown
账号定位：产地直发水果账号，用真实产地证明 + 轻娱乐主播包装 + 朴素信任感成交。
人设组合：年轻主播负责停留和亲近感，产地工人/果农负责真实感，长辈角色负责信任背书。
核心承诺：新鲜、好吃、产地真实、价格有理由、坏果售后清楚。
成交动作：评论口令、私信、进橱窗/直播间、关注等下次开箱。
```

## Content Pillars

Use three pillars in rotation:

1. `诱惑感`: fruit close-up, cut fruit, juice, chewing reaction, sweet/crisp/sour balance.
2. `真实感`: orchard, workers picking, sorting, packing, bad果筛掉, overnight shipping.
3. `信任感`: older farmer/老太太/老板娘 simple sales pitch, price reason, guarantee, repeat customer proof.

Avoid letting the account become only "美女跳舞". Beauty and dance can pull attention, but the fruit must become the reason to stay and buy.

## Best Starting Formats

Read `references/content-playbook.md` when building a batch plan. Default batch:

- 30% orchard/proof videos.
- 30% eating/reaction videos.
- 20% plainspoken elder/farmer pitch.
- 20% light entertainment or dance with fruit.

## Execution Rules

- Make one fruit variety the weekly hero. Do not switch too fast before the account learns an audience.
- Use one visible proof per video: cut open, hand squeeze, sweetness test, harvest date, packing line, or customer unboxing.
- Keep claims conservative unless evidence is visible. Say "这批口感偏甜" rather than "全网最甜".
- Build recurring labels: `今天这筐`, `果园刚摘`, `老板娘试吃`, `坏果不发`, `一口爆汁`.
- Keep each video to one conversion question: `要不要我拍打包过程`, `想看哪种果`, `要小果还是大果`.
