# Fruit Commerce Content Playbook

## Four Repeatable Columns

### 1. Beauty Host Eating Fruit

Purpose: retention and repeat view.

Formula:

```markdown
0-2s: 主播手拿水果靠近镜头，第一口或切开瞬间。
2-6s: 表情反应，不要只夸甜，要说具体口感。
6-10s: 镜头给果肉、汁水、大小、果皮。
10-15s: 轻 CTA：想看打包/产地/价格就评论。
```

Good uses:

- Eating mango, lychee, orange, grape, peach, melon.
- Light dance only after the fruit appears in the first two seconds.
- Works well with RunningHub action transfer if a clean dance or hand-movement reference exists.

Risk:

- If the fruit is only a prop, conversion will be weak.
- If the dance is too nightclub-like, trust and commerce intent drop.

### 2. Orchard Origin Proof

Purpose: trust and supply-chain proof.

Formula:

```markdown
0-2s: 树上果、采摘动作、满筐果。
2-6s: 工人忙着摘/选/装箱。
6-10s: 展示坏果筛选、果径、成熟度。
10-15s: 老板/果农一句真实说明。
```

Good uses:

- "这筐今天刚下树"
- "小果更甜还是大果更甜"
- "为什么这批有花皮但更香"

### 3. Plain Elder Pitch

Purpose: trust, warmth, non-advertising feeling.

Formula:

```markdown
0-3s: 老太太/老板娘拿一筐果，不用精致包装。
3-8s: 一句朴素理由：今年雨水/日照/采摘时间。
8-12s: 切开或递给镜头。
12-15s: 轻声 CTA：想要我给你挑一筐好的。
```

Avoid:

- Fake poverty selling.
- Overly scripted tragic stories.
- Medical or exaggerated nutrition claims.

### 4. Packing And After-Sale Proof

Purpose: reduce purchase anxiety.

Formula:

```markdown
0-2s: 一箱水果打开或正在打包。
2-7s: 展示分拣、称重、防撞包装。
7-12s: 解释坏果/运输/售后标准。
12-15s: 评论区问规格或地区。
```

## Weekly Cadence

- Day 1: orchard proof.
- Day 2: host eating reaction.
- Day 3: packing/quality control.
- Day 4: elder/farmer pitch.
- Day 5: light entertainment/dance.
- Day 6: customer question answer.
- Day 7: recap and offer.

## Metrics

Watch first:

- 3-second hold rate: hook/cover quality.
- Average watch duration: beat pacing.
- Comment intent: product curiosity.
- Profile clicks: trust and offer.
- Sales/conversation rate: price, stock, logistics, customer service.
