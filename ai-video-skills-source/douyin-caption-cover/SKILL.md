# Douyin Caption Cover

## Overview

Use this skill once the video topic or final cut is known. Build a publish-ready package that sells the click honestly and makes the cover, title, and body point to the same viewer promise.

## Inputs

Use what is available:

- Final video, draft, transcript, or production brief.
- Account persona and target audience.
- Product/service/offer and forbidden claims.
- Desired tone: tutorial, candid, soft sell, hard sell, review, story, list, or case.

If current tags, platform wording, or trend references need research, prefer `jina-search` when available.

## Package Structure

Generate:

- 3 title options: direct benefit, curiosity, and scenario/pain-point.
- 1 recommended title with reason.
- 1 main caption and 1 shorter fallback.
- 5-12 hashtags: mix niche, category, scene, and branded/account tags.
- 3 cover options: frame choice, cover text, layout direction.
- 1 compliance note: claims to soften or avoid.

## Copy Rules

- Make the first line concrete; avoid vague motivational openings.
- Use the same key phrase across cover/title/caption when possible.
- Keep cover text short enough for mobile thumbnail reading.
- Do not invent results, prices, credentials, before/after proof, or scarcity.
- Do not over-tag unrelated trending topics.
- For commerce content, make the viewer problem and product role clear without sounding like a banner ad.

Use `references/caption-cover-patterns.md` for patterns and output format.
