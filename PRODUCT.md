# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Chinese university students initially reached through ANS, CAUHub, class groups and campus media; later creators and AI adopters across universities. Many are beginners and need concrete outputs, personal inputs and a few clear decisions rather than exposed engineering details.

## Product Purpose
ANS organizes real projects, university or topic Hubs and mature reusable workflows. Discover an outcome, enter personal facts, confirm the actual run cost, obtain and manage genuine artifacts. Creators can distribute reviewed runnable workflows in the marketplace. Six primary routes are project square, Hubs/workflows, community growth, events, market and workspace; favorites and compute belong in the account menu.

## Brand Personality
Approachable, playful, capable. Original talkative cute dinosaur helps when asked and stays quiet during focused work. The owner requires current real content and dislikes verbose placeholder claims, invented project numbers and the old prompt repository identity.

## Anti-references
Old sprawling prompt-platform navigation, decorative dashboard metrics, fake members/reviews/outcomes, technical jargon in beginner journeys, static pages claiming execution without actual artifacts, cloned third-party mascot identity.

## Design Principles
Show the real outcome first. Keep navigation finite and predictable. Separate discovery categories from runnable recipes and private user work. Reuse fixed code and reserve AI for personalized content. Explicit cost confirmation before execution. Keep content honest and user work private.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Existing project keyboard focus, Radix semantics, responsive mobile layout, multilingual strings and reduced-motion/static dinosaur fallback are confirmed requirements. Keep existing logout/session/unsaved-work safeguards. Use clear labels and readable contrast; do not claim a formal accessibility certification.
